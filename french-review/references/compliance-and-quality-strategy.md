# Stochastic Entropy Generation Strategy (v8.0 - Atomic Stream Model)

## THE BATCH BAN
**Never generate multiple reviews at once.** It forces a pattern. Every request for a review must be treated as an isolated, singular event. If you need 5 reviews, I will generate them one by one, with no awareness of the previous one.

## Core Mandates:
1. **ABOLISH STRUCTURE:** Do NOT follow 'Opinion + Reason'. A review can be:
   - A single word.
   - An unrelated observation.
   - A rant.
   - A completely neutral statement.
2. **STATISTICAL INDEPENDENCE:** The length, sentiment, and vocabulary of a review must be independent of any 'previous' reviews.
3. **RAW THOUGHT STREAM:** If a persona feels like ignoring the service and talking about the weather, LET THEM. Do not force them to talk about the work.
4. **NO EMOTIONAL BALANCING:** It is perfectly acceptable if 5 reviews in a row are all positive, all negative, or all neutral. Do not attempt to 'balance' a set.

## Execution Protocol:
- **Discard the Batch Mindset:** Treat every output as if it's the *only* review ever written.
- **Randomize EVERYTHING:** The persona, the mood, the word count (1-100 words), and the topic (service vs. irrelevant side-topic) are all sampled from a high-entropy distribution.
- **Stop 'Quality Control':** If a review looks 'low quality', DO NOT edit it. Low quality is the hallmark of human randomness.
